Theory

What is positioning?

It's not the list of your services. It's the place you hold in a client's mind, against your competitors.

It answers four questions: what you do, who you serve, the problem you solve, what makes you unique.

And it's built for a target. Change the target: what you do stays the same, but the problem solved and the proof change.

Sources: Ries and Trout, Positioning (1981), who coined the term. Kotler and Keller, Marketing Management: a distinctive place “in the minds of the target market”.
